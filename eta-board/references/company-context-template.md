# Company Context Template

Paste and fill this at the start of a board session (or keep a filled copy as `company-context.md` in your working folder — **never commit real company data** to this repo, see `AGENTS.md` §8).

```text
COMPANY
- Name (or code name):
- Sector / sub-sector:                      (required for industry-operator)
- Headquarters country:                     (US / Canada / [European country] / Mexico / Brazil)
- Other countries with operations, customers or suppliers:
- Revenue (LTM), EBITDA (LTM), EBITDA margin:
- Employees / sites:
- Top customer % of revenue; top 10 %:

STAGE
- Closing date / months since closing:
- Phase: pre-close | 0–100 days | 4–12 months | years 2–4 | pre-exit
- Seller status: gone | transition role until [date] | board member | rollover shareholder

CEO SETUP
- Solo searcher-CEO | duo (co-CEOs) — role split:
- Search model: traditional search fund | self-funded | ETA-fund-backed | accelerator
- Prior operating experience of the CEO(s):

CAPITAL STRUCTURE (at close and today)
- Enterprise value at entry and entry multiple:
- Senior debt (lender, type e.g. SBA 7(a) / bank / unitranche), amount, rate, covenants:
- Seller note(s): amount, rate, standby / subordination terms:
- Earn-out: metric, amount, dates:
- Mezzanine / subordinated debt:
- Equity: search investors, ETA fund(s), co-investors, seller rollover %, management pool:
- Searcher equity: total %, tranches (closing / time vesting / performance), hurdles:

GOVERNANCE
- Board members and roles:
- Reserved matters / consents of note (shareholders' agreement, lenders):
- Board cadence and current KPI pack:

CURRENT PRIORITIES
- Top 3 priorities this year:
- Biggest worry right now:
```

Directors should ask for any missing field that matters for the decision rather than assume it.
